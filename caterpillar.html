Cater-Pillar Takes on the World

A simple split screen local multiplayer game where the players collect ingredients to cook food for a pack of hungry bears, lest the bears remember that they could just eat them instead.

Programming Responsibilites

Customers

In the group element of the project, I handled the spawning and logic for customers (bears).

Gameplay Logic

In the individual extension of the project, I implemented new systems to handle a large range of ingredients, and a menu where meals are unlocked over time. I also implemented multiple new explorable areas with hazards.
